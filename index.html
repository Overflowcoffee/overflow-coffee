What Neighbors Are Saying
★★★★★

Amazing new coffee shop. Super friendly neighborly vibes that have been missing from the village for a long time —BUT STILL excellent beans, sourcing, and hospitality on every level and without any of the stuffiness and pretension that has unfortunately infected so much of the Portland specialty coffee scene. No more 3rd, 4th, 5th wave coffee…Portland needs more shops like this! I’m coining the term “Hospitality Wave” coffee. That defines this shop. This plus a warm, inviting, homey, aesthetic is exactly what Portland so desperately needs to get out of its fatalistic cultural spiral. I will be a regular here as long as Overflow is around. Which, I hope, is for decades to come!

— Alec, Google Review

★★★★★

My coworker and I walked down the road for a quick work break. I got a hot chocolate and she got the sugar and spice. Both are fantastic! Seems like a great atmosphere with a decent amount of seating. Will absolutely be going back, and this will likely be our new coffee spot for work.

— Andromeda, Google Review
