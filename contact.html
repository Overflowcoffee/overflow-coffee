We're right in the heart of Multnomah Village. Grab your drink and wander. Here are a few neighbors worth a visit.

Independent bookstore, since 1978. Grab a coffee, then browse.
Gabriel Park
91 acres with an off-leash area, play structures and picnic spots. Take your cup for a walk.
Thinker Toys
A hands-on toy store. A good stop with the kids.
Northwest Wools
Portland's oldest yarn store, with hand-dyed fibers from regional artists.
